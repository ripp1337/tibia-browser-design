# Crafting System

## Overview

Crafting is one of the primary progression systems in the game.

Crafting exists to:

- Support Combat Progression
- Provide an Alternative Progression Path
- Create Value From Gathered Materials
- Produce Valuable Consumables
- Produce High-Demand Utility Items

Crafting is both:

- A Major Progression Pillar
- An Important Endgame Activity

---

# Crafting Philosophy

The Crafting System supports the game's progression ecosystem.

Crafting enhances:

- Combat
- Resource Management
- Economic Progression
- Long-Term Planning

Crafting does not replace:

- Loot Hunting
- Equipment Acquisition
- Character Progression

---

## Equipment Philosophy

Crafting never directly creates equipment.

Equipment enters the economy exclusively through:

- Monster Loot
- Boss Loot
- Gambling
- Trading

Crafting supports equipment progression through utility items rather than equipment generation.

---
