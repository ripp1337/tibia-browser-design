# 5. ECONOMY

## Overview

The economy is built around three primary resources:

- Gold
- Energy
- Materials

Each resource has a different purpose:

Gold:

- Character development
- Upgrades
- Trading
- Gambling
- Long-term progression

Energy:

- Limits combat activity
- Controls progression speed
- Regenerates automatically

Materials:

- Crafting
- Equipment upgrades
- Protection systems

The economy is designed so that gold always remains valuable, even for endgame players.

---

# Gold

## Gold Sources

Players may obtain gold from:

- Monster kills
- Boss kills
- Hourly Chests
- Daily Chests
- Selling items to NPC vendors
- Selling items to other players

### Monster Rewards

Every monster has a predefined gold reward range stored in the database.

Example:

Monster:

Gold Min

Gold Max

Actual reward is rolled within the configured range.

Gold rewards can be increased through active Gold Boosts.

---

## Gold Sinks

Gold leaves the economy through:

- Equipment upgrades
- Upgrade protection
- Gambling
- Crafting
- NPC vendors
- NPC healing
- Blessing purchases
- Spell slot unlocks
- Temporary boosts
- Auction House listing fees

---

# Energy System

Energy is the primary gameplay resource.

Energy exists to:

- Limit unlimited combat
- Control progression speed
- Encourage resource management

---

## Energy Capacity

Base Energy:

- 100

Energy Growth:

- +5 Energy every 20 character levels

Maximum:

- 200 Energy at level 100

---

## Energy Regeneration

Regeneration Rate:

- 1 Energy per minute

Daily Energy Generation:

- 1,440 Energy

Energy continues regenerating while online and offline.

---

## Energy Consumption

Regular Monster Fight:

- 3 Energy

Boss Fight:

- 5 Energy

Energy is consumed when a fight begins.

---

## Energy Potions

Energy Potions are rare consumables.

Sources:

- Loot
- Crafting

Energy Potions cannot be purchased from NPC vendors.

### Potion Types

Small Energy Potion:

- Restores 10 Energy

Medium Energy Potion:

- Restores 20 Energy

Large Energy Potion:

- Restores 30 Energy

---

# Gambling System

Gambling allows players to exchange gold for randomly generated equipment.

Purpose:

- Major gold sink
- Alternative equipment progression path
- Excitement and chance-based gameplay

---

## Gambling Rules

Gambling generates:

1. Random Item Base
2. Random Rarity
3. Random Affixes

Rules:

- Guaranteed minimum Magic rarity
- Can roll Rare, Epic and Legendary items
- Uses the normal item generation system

---

## Gambling Costs

Weapon Gamble:

- 10,000 Gold

Armor Gamble:

- 5,000 Gold

Armor includes:

- Helmet
- Armor
- Shield
- Legs
- Boots

Jewelry Gamble:

- 25,000 Gold

Jewelry includes:

- Ring
- Amulet

---

## Gambling Rarity Distribution

Recommended default chances:

Magic:

- 80%

Rare:

- 15%

Epic:

- 4%

Legendary:

- 1%

These values may be balanced later.

---

Blessings may be:

- Purchased
- Crafted
- Looted

Character Level 21-30:

- 10,000 Gold

- 20,000 Gold

- 50,000 Gold

# Promotion

Promotion is a one-time permanent upgrade.

Requirements:

- Level 20
- 20,000 Gold

Effect:

- Reduces death experience loss from 10% to 8%

---

# Temporary Boost Economy

Temporary boosts may be:

- Crafted
- Looted
- Purchased (selected types)

Boosts do not stack.

Only one boost of the same category may be active at a time.

---

## Gold Boosts

Sources:

- Loot
- Crafting
- Chests
- Level Rewards

Cannot be purchased from NPCs



Gold Boosts increase gold rewards from combat.

Boosts are measured in completed fights, not time.

Only one Gold Boost may be active at a time.

Examples:

Small Gold Boost

+10% Gold

100 Fights

---

Medium Gold Boost

+15% Gold

250 Fights

---

Large Gold Boost

+25% Gold

500 Fights

---

# NPC Vendors

NPC Vendors represent an unlimited economy source and sink.

NPC stock is infinite.

---

## NPC Vendors Sell

- Common equipment
- Potions
- Basic materials
- Blessings
- Experience boosts

NPC equipment is intended mainly for early-game progression.

Most mid-game and end-game equipment comes from:

- Loot
- Bosses
- Crafting
- Gambling
- Trading

---

## Equipment Pricing

NPC equipment value:

Item Level × 100 Gold

Example:

Level 10 Item:

- 1,000 Gold

Level 50 Item:

- 5,000 Gold

Level 100 Item:

- 10,000 Gold

---

## Material Pricing

Basic materials can be purchased.

Formula:

Material Tier × Constant

Higher-tier materials are substantially more expensive.

Rare materials are generally unavailable from vendors.

---

# NPC Healing

NPC Healers restore health in exchange for gold.

Rules:

- Restore up to 50% of missing health
- Available once every 60 minutes

Pricing:

Level 1-30:

- Level × 100 Gold

Level 31-50:

- Level × 150 Gold

Level 51-80:

- Level × 200 Gold

Level 81-100:

- Level × 300 Gold

Level 101+:

- Level × 500 Gold

---

# Equipment Upgrade Economy

Equipment upgrading is one of the largest gold sinks in the game.

Upgrades require:

- Gold
- Materials

Upgrade cost scales based on:

- Item Level
- Current Item Rarity

---

## Upgrade Protection

Players may purchase upgrade protection.

Protection prevents item destruction.

Protection requires:

- Gold
- Protection Stone

Protection Stones:

- Cannot be purchased
- Can be crafted
- Can be looted

Protection cost scales with:

- Item Level
- Current Item Rarity

Upgrade Protection is expected to be one of the largest endgame gold sinks.

---

# Salvaging Economy

Items may be salvaged instead of sold.

Salvage rewards depend on:

- Item Level
- Item Rarity

Higher rarities produce more valuable materials.

Players may choose to:

- Equip item
- Sell item
- Trade item
- Salvage item

---

# Vendor Sell Value

Items sold to NPC vendors use Item Score.

Formula:

Item Score × 100 Gold

Item Score is determined by:

- Item Level
- Base Stats
- Rarity
- Affixes
- Affix Values

Better items generate higher vendor values.

---

# Auction House

Players may trade freely.

---

## Listing Rules

Maximum Active Listings:

- 10 per character

Listing Duration:

- 24 Hours

Expired listings return to the player.

---

## Listing Cost

5% of asking price

Minimum:

- 100 Gold

Listing fee is paid immediately and is not refunded.

---

## Sale Tax

No additional sale tax.

Only the listing fee removes gold from the economy.

---

# Economic Philosophy

The economy is designed around:

- Steady gold generation through combat
- Continuous gold destruction through upgrades
- Valuable player trading
- Long-term progression goals
- Meaningful item chasing

Primary Endgame Gold Sinks:

1. Equipment Upgrading
2. Upgrade Protection
3. Blessings
4. Gambling

These systems are intentionally designed to remove massive amounts of gold from wealthy players and keep the economy healthy over time.
