# Season Design
## Tibia Browser RPG

---

# 1. Overview

The game supports two parallel endgame paths:

1. Seasonal Competition
2. Account Completion

Seasonal players compete for leaderboard rankings.

Completion-focused players pursue:

- Holy Grail Completion
- Achievement Completion
- Long-Term Collection Growth

The game is intentionally designed to support both play styles.

---

# 2. Seasonal Structure

## Season Duration

Each season lasts:

3 Months

At season end:

- Leaderboards are finalized
- Seasonal rankings are archived
- Characters transfer to the Non-Ladder Realm
- A new season begins

---

# 3. New Season Rules

Every new season requires:

- Creation of a New Character
- Fresh Progression
- Fresh Economy
- Fresh Leaderboards

Nothing transfers into the new season.

Every season begins from zero.

---

# 4. Leaderboards

## Main Seasonal Leaderboard

The primary competition is:

Experience Leaderboard

Ranking is determined by:

1. Highest Character Level
2. Highest Experience

The player with the most experience at season end is the winner.

---

## Character-Based Rankings

Leaderboards rank:

Characters

not

Accounts.

Each seasonal character competes independently.

---

## Hardcore Leaderboard

A separate Hardcore Leaderboard exists.

Requirement:

- No Deaths

Players who maintain a death-free character compete independently from the main ladder.

Hardcore rankings are prestige-based only.

---

# 5. Seasonal Winners

## First Place

Season Champion

Rewards:

- Hall of Fame Entry
- Top 1 Achievement

The achievement remains attached to the character permanently.

---

## Top 10

Top 10 players receive:

Strong Contender Achievement

This acts as permanent proof of seasonal success.

---

## Other Rankings

There are:

- No Seasonal Items
- No Seasonal Power Rewards
- No Future-Season Bonuses

Seasons are intended to remain fair and self-contained.

---

# 6. Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame permanently records:

- Season Number
- Character Name
- Final Level
- Final Experience
- Season Placement

The Hall of Fame serves as the permanent historical record of competitive play.

---

# 7. Seasonal Economy

Every season operates with its own economy.

Seasonal players may only trade with:

- Seasonal Players

The seasonal marketplace and economy are completely separate from the Non-Ladder Realm.

---

# 8. Non-Ladder Realm

## Character Transfer

At season end, characters transfer exactly as they currently exist.

Transferred Character Data:

- Levels
- Experience
- Equipment
- Gold
- Inventory
- Spell Mastery
- Crafting Level
- Gathering Level
- Character Statistics

Character progression is preserved completely.

---

## Account-Wide Systems

The following systems are Account-Wide and remain unaffected by seasons:

- Achievements
- Achievement Score
- Holy Grail Progress
- Outfit Collection
- Addon Collection

These systems remain permanently available across all seasons and all characters.

---

## Non-Ladder Gameplay

Transferred characters remain fully playable forever.

Players may continue:

- Fighting Monsters
- Farming Loot
- Completing Sets
- Trading
- Crafting
- Completing Achievements

The Non-Ladder Realm acts as a permanent archive for completed seasonal characters.

---

# 9. Character Slots

Accounts may have:

Maximum:

3 Character Slots

When a seasonal character finishes:

The character remains on the account.

Characters may be:

- Active
- Archived

Archived characters become:

View-Only

and free a character slot.

Archiving never deletes progress.

---

# 10. Non-Ladder Economy

All transferred characters enter the same Non-Ladder Realm.

All Non-Ladder characters share:

- Marketplace
- Economy
- Trading

Every completed season contributes characters to the same permanent world.

---

# 11. Catch-Up Philosophy

There are no catch-up mechanics.

Late-season players receive:

- No Bonus Experience
- No Bonus Loot
- No Bonus Progression

All players compete under identical conditions.

---

# 12. Seasonal Content

Current seasonal gameplay focuses on:

- Fresh Start
- Fresh Economy
- Fresh Leaderboards

There are currently:

- No Special Seasonal Monsters
- No Special Seasonal Bosses
- No Seasonal Modifiers
- No Seasonal Objectives

Future seasons may optionally introduce new content.

---

# 13. Achievement Persistence

Achievements are Account-Wide.

Achievements:

- Never Reset
- Remain Permanent
- Persist Across All Seasons

This includes:

- Achievement Progress
- Achievement Score
- Achievement Unlocks

Seasonal resets never affect achievements.

---

# 14. Holy Grail Persistence

Holy Grail Progress is Account-Wide.

Every discovered:

- Unique Item
- Set Item

is permanently recorded.

Progress remains regardless of:

- Character
- Season
- Item Loss
- Trading
- Salvaging

Examples:

Holy Grail:

45%

---

Holy Grail:

82%

---

Holy Grail:

100%

Holy Grail progress never resets.

---

# 15. Endgame Goals

## Casual Endgame

Typical seasonal goals:

- Reach Level 100
- Improve Equipment
- Complete Achievements
- Build Holy Grail Progress

---

## Competitive Endgame

Competitive players focus on:

- Top 10 Placement
- Top 1 Placement
- Hardcore Rankings

Success is measured by leaderboard position.

---

## Completionist Endgame

Completion-focused players pursue:

- Holy Grail Completion
- Achievement Completion
- Set Collection
- Unique Collection

Progress accumulates across all seasons.

---

# 16. Ultimate Seasonal Goal

Become:

Season Champion

Finish:

#1 on the Experience Leaderboard

and earn a permanent Hall of Fame Entry.

---

# 17. Ultimate Long-Term Goal

Complete the Holy Grail.

Requirement:

Discover every Unique Item and Set Item in the game.

This achievement spans:

- Multiple Characters
- Multiple Seasons
- Entire Account History

The Holy Grail represents the ultimate completionist objective.

---

# 18. Endgame Philosophy

The game supports two parallel progression paths.

Competitive Path:

Season

↓

Race

↓

Leaderboard

↓

Hall of Fame

---

Completionist Path:

Holy Grail

↓

Achievements

↓

Collections

↓

Permanent Account Progress

Neither path invalidates the other.

Players are free to focus on:

- Competition
- Completion
- Both Simultaneously
