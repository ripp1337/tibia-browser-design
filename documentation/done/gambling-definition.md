# GAMBLING DEFINITION

## Overview

Gambling allows players to exchange gold for randomly generated equipment.

Purpose:

- Major Gold Sink
- Alternative Equipment Progression Path
- Excitement and Chance-Based Gameplay

Equipment generated through Gambling uses the normal item generation system.

---

# Gambling Rules

Gambling generates:

1. Random Item Base
2. Random Rarity
3. Random Affixes

Rules:

- Guaranteed minimum Magic rarity
- Can roll Rare items
- Can roll Epic items
- Can roll Legendary items
- Uses the normal item generation system

---

# Item Generation

Gambling follows the standard equipment generation flow.

Process:

Item Base

↓

Rarity

↓

Affixes

↓

Final Item

Generated items use the same systems as normal equipment:

- Item Bases
- Rarity Generation
- Affix Generation
- Affix Rolls

- Common Items

Minimum rarity:

- Magic

Maximum rarity:

- Legendary

---

# Generated Equipment

Generated equipment follows all normal equipment rules.

Generated items may contain:

- Item Level
- Item Base Statistics
- Rarity
- Affixes
- Affix Rolls

Generated items are normal equipment items and may be:

- Equipped
- Sold
- Traded
- Salvaged
- Upgraded

---

# Economy

Gambling is one of the primary Gold Sinks in the game.

Gold leaves the economy through:

- Equipment Upgrades
- Upgrade Protection
- Gambling
- Crafting
- NPC Vendors
- NPC Healing
- Blessing Purchases
- Spell Slot Unlocks
- Temporary Boosts
- Auction House Listing Fees

Primary Endgame Gold Sinks:

1. Equipment Upgrading
2. Upgrade Protection
3. Blessings
4. Gambling

The system is intentionally designed to remove large amounts of gold from the economy while providing an alternative path to equipment acquisition.

---

# Equipment Sources

Equipment may enter the economy through:

- Monster Loot
- Boss Loot
- Gambling
- Trading

Gambling is one of the primary equipment acquisition systems.

---

# Design Philosophy

Gambling exists to provide:

Gold

↓

Random Equipment

↓

Progression Opportunity

The system serves as:

- A major gold sink
- An alternative equipment progression path
- A source of excitement through randomness

Generated equipment uses the same item generation systems as all other equipment in the game.
