# UPGRADE SYSTEM

## Overview

Equipment may be improved through two separate systems:

1. Rarity Upgrading
2. Affix Rerolling

These systems are completely independent.

Equipment progression is driven by:

- Loot
- Boss Rewards
- Gambling
- Crafting
- Trading
- Upgrading

---

# Rarity Upgrading

## Overview

Rarity Upgrading increases an item's rarity and adds new affixes.

Progression:

Common

↓

Magic

↓

Rare

↓

Epic

↓

Legendary

Each successful upgrade grants one additional affix.

Examples:

Common:

- 0 Affixes

Magic:

- 1 Affix

Rare:

- 2 Affixes

Epic:

- 3 Affixes

Legendary:

- 4 Affixes

Legendary is the maximum rarity.

Further rarity upgrades are not possible.

---

# Success Chances

Default success rates:

Common → Magic:

- 80%

Magic → Rare:

- 50%

Rare → Epic:

- 25%

Epic → Legendary:

- 10%

These values are configurable and may be adjusted during balancing.

---

# Upgrade Requirements

Every rarity upgrade requires:

- Gold
- Upgrade Materials

Upgrade costs scale with:

- Item Level
- Current Rarity

Higher-level and higher-rarity items require significantly greater investment.

---

# Upgrade Failure

Failed rarity upgrades result in:

- Item Destroyed
- Materials Consumed
- Gold Consumed

The item is permanently lost.

---

# Upgrade Protection

## Protection Stones

Players may use a Protection Stone before attempting an upgrade.

Rules:

- Protection Stone is consumed
- Gold is consumed
- Materials are consumed

On Failure:

- Item survives
- Upgrade fails

On Success:

- Item upgrades normally

Protection Stones protect only a single upgrade attempt.

---

## Protection Stone Sources

Protection Stones may be obtained through:

- Crafting
- Loot
- Boss Loot

Protection Stones cannot be purchased from vendors.

---

## Protection Stone Usage

Single upgrade attempt.

Protection Stone is consumed immediately.

Outcome:

Success:

- Stone consumed

Failure:

- Stone consumed

Regardless of result:

- Stone disappears

---

## Variants

None.

Only one Protection Stone exists in the game.

---

# Affix Rerolling

## Overview

Affix Rerolling allows players to improve specific affixes on an item.

The player selects exactly one affix to reroll.

---

## Example

Before:

Attack +52

Attack +41

Gold +2%

Mana +47

Selected:

Gold +2%

After:

Attack

...

The selected affix is rerolled while the remaining affixes stay unchanged.

---

# Upgrade Economy

Equipment upgrading is one of the largest Gold Sinks in the game.

Upgrades require:

- Gold
- Materials

Upgrade cost scales based on:

- Item Level
- Current Item Rarity

---

# Upgrade Protection Economy

Players may purchase upgrade protection.

Protection prevents item destruction.

Protection requires:

- Gold
- Protection Stone

Protection cost scales with:

- Item Level
- Current Item Rarity

Upgrade Protection is expected to be one of the largest endgame Gold Sinks.

---

# Material Usage

Materials are primarily used for:

- Crafting
- Equipment Upgrades
- Protection Crafting
- Blessing Crafting

Material progression is level-based.

---

# Equipment Progression Integration

Equipment may be:

- Looted
- Purchased
- Traded
- Gambled
- Upgraded
- Salvaged

Equipment upgrading is one of the primary methods of long-term item improvement.

---

# Design Philosophy

Equipment improvement follows:

Item

↓

Rarity Upgrade

↓

Additional Affixes

↓

Higher Item Power

or

Item

↓

Affix Rerolling

↓

Improved Affix Quality

↓

Higher Item Power

The strongest equipment is expected to result from a combination of:

- High Item Level
- Legendary Rarity
- Strong Affix Tiers
- High Affix Rolls
- Successful Upgrades

Equipment upgrading exists as a major progression system and one of the largest long-term Gold Sinks in the game.
