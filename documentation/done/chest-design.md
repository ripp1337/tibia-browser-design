# Chest System

## Overview

Chests are recurring rewards designed to encourage regular logins and provide supplemental progression.

Chests are not intended to replace:

- Combat
- Loot Hunting
- Crafting
- Equipment Progression

The system exists to:

- Encourage player retention
- Provide small progression boosts
- Reward regular activity
- Support the core gameplay loop

Chests should feel rewarding without becoming the primary progression source.

---

# Chest Structure

The system consists of three components:

- Hourly Chest
- Daily Chest
- Login Streak Rewards

Each component serves a different purpose within the retention system.

---

# Design Philosophy

Core progression should always remain:

Combat

↓

Loot

↓

Crafting

↓

Equipment

Chests exist to support this progression path rather than replace it.

They are intended to provide:

- Supplemental rewards
- Login incentives
- Small progression acceleration
- Additional excitement

---

# Hourly Chest

## Overview

Players may claim one Hourly Chest every 60 minutes.

The cooldown begins when the previous chest is opened.

---

## Charging Rules

Example:

Claim Chest

↓

60 Minutes Pass

↓

Next Chest Available

---

If the chest is not claimed:

- The chest remains available
- No second chest is generated
- The timer does not restart

Only one Hourly Chest may be waiting at a time.

---

## Storage Rules

Hourly Chests do not stack.

Maximum stored:

- 1 Hourly Chest

Players must claim the available chest before a new timer begins.

---

## Reward Philosophy

Hourly Chests should provide:

- Small Gold Rewards
- Small Material Rewards
- Small Consumable Rewards

Hourly Chests should feel useful but never mandatory.

---

# Hourly Chest Rewards

## Guaranteed Gold

Every Hourly Chest contains Gold.

Gold scales with:

- Character Level

This ensures rewards remain useful throughout progression.

---

## Materials

Hourly Chests may contain materials.

Material selection is limited to:

- Materials available within the character's current Gathering progression

Materials are selected randomly from the eligible pool.

---

## Equipment

Hourly Chests may generate equipment.

Equipment generation follows the Gambling System.

Generation Flow:

Item Base

↓

Item Level

↓

Rarity

↓

Affixes

↓

Final Item

---

## Equipment Rarity Distribution

Hourly Chests use Gambling rarity chances.

### Magic

80%

---

### Rare

15%

---

### Epic

4%

---

### Legendary

1%

---

### Restrictions

Hourly Chests never generate:

- Common Equipment

---

## Consumables

Possible consumable rewards:

- Small Energy Potions
- Small Gold Boosts
- Small Experience Boosts

Consumables are intentionally uncommon.

---

## Restricted Rewards

Hourly Chests cannot contain:

- Blessings
- Protection Stones

These rewards are reserved for higher-value systems.

---

# Daily Chest

## Overview

Players may claim one Daily Chest every 24 hours.

The cooldown begins when the previous Daily Chest is opened.

---

## Timing Example

Claim Time:

10:00

↓

Next Daily Chest:

10:00 Next Day

---

## Storage Rules

Daily Chests do not stack.

Maximum stored:

- 1 Daily Chest

Players must claim the current reward before another becomes available.

---

## Reward Philosophy

Daily Chests should feel meaningful and exciting.

They are intended to provide:

- Valuable rewards
- Consistent account progression
- Retention incentives

Daily Chests should remain weaker than highly efficient active gameplay.

---

# Daily Chest Rewards

## Guaranteed Gold

Every Daily Chest contains Gold.

Gold scales with:

- Character Level

---

## Guaranteed Equipment

Every Daily Chest grants one guaranteed equipment item.

### Item 1

Minimum Quality:

Rare or Better

Rarity Distribution:

#### Rare

75%

#### Epic

20%

#### Legendary

5%

---

### Restrictions

The guaranteed equipment reward cannot generate:

- Rings
- Amulets

---

# Special Equipment Roll

A second independent reward roll exists.

### Chance

2%

---

### Result

If successful:

- Unique Item

OR

- Set Item

This roll is completely independent from all other Daily Chest rewards.

---

# Daily Chest Item Levels

Equipment levels are generated relative to character progression.

Formula:

Character Level -5

to

Character Level +10

---

### Example

Character Level:

50

Possible Item Levels:

45 - 60

---

# Material Rewards

Daily Chests contain materials.

Characteristics:

- Progression Appropriate
- Larger Quantities Than Hourly Chests

Materials are selected from progression-valid reward pools.

---

# Blessings

Daily Chests may contain Blessings.

### Chance

15%

### Reward

- 1 Blessing

---

# Protection Stones

Daily Chests may contain Protection Stones.

### Chance

10%

### Reward

- 1 Protection Stone

---

# Energy Potions

Daily Chests may contain:

- Medium Energy Potions
- Large Energy Potions

Small Energy Potions are excluded from Daily Chest rewards.

---

# Boost Rewards

Daily Chests may award:

- Gold Boosts
- Experience Boosts

All boost tiers are eligible.

Examples:

- Small Boost
- Medium Boost
- Large Boost

---

# Login Streak System

## Overview

Players receive additional rewards for consecutive daily logins.

The system encourages regular play and consistency.

---

## Core Rules

Missing a day immediately resets the streak.

Maximum streak length:

- 7 Days

After Day 7:

- Reward is granted
- Streak resets
- Progress begins again at Day 1

---

# Day 1 Reward

Reward:

- Random Magic Item

---

# Day 2 Reward

Reward:

- Random Rare Item

---

# Day 3 Reward

Reward:

- Random Epic Item

Restrictions:

- No Rings
- No Amulets

---

# Day 4 Reward

Reward:

- Random Epic Ring

OR

- Random Epic Amulet

---

# Day 5 Reward

Rewards:

- Large Gold Boost
- Large Experience Boost

---

# Day 6 Reward

Rewards:

- Protection Stone
- Additional Daily Chest

---

# Day 7 Reward

Rewards:

- Blessing
- Additional Daily Chest

---

# Reward Scaling Philosophy

Chest rewards scale through:

- Character Level
- Progression Access
- Eligible Material Pools
- Item Level Range

The system should remain relevant throughout the game's lifecycle.

Lower-level players and endgame players should both receive useful rewards appropriate to their progression stage.

---

# Player Retention Goals

The Chest System exists to encourage:

- Hourly Logins
- Daily Logins
- Consecutive Login Streaks
- Long-Term Engagement

The system should promote regular activity without making players feel forced to log in.

---

# Reward Restrictions

Chests must never become the optimal progression strategy.

Players should never progress faster through:

- Chests
- Login Rewards
- Passive Collection

than through:

- Combat
- Loot Farming
- Boss Encounters
- Crafting
- Trading

---

# System Relationships

The Chest System supplements:

- Combat Progression
- Loot Acquisition
- Crafting Progression
- Economy Progression

The system does not replace any of them.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Chest System exists to create:

Login

↓

Claim Reward

↓

Small Progress Gain

↓

Continue Playing

The ideal player experience is:

Hourly Chest

→ Nice Bonus

Daily Chest

→ Exciting Reward

Login Streak

→ Long-Term Motivation

The system should provide consistent value while ensuring that the primary source of progression remains active gameplay.
