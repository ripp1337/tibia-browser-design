# Marketplace System

## Overview

The Marketplace is the primary player-to-player trading system in the game.

All trading occurs through the Marketplace.

Direct player trading does not exist.

The Marketplace exists to:

- Create a Player-Driven Economy
- Enable Item Trading
- Support Build Optimization
- Support Crafting Progression
- Create Long-Term Economic Value

The Marketplace is one of the core pillars of the game's economy.

---

# Marketplace Philosophy

The Marketplace exists to connect supply and demand.

Players obtain items through:

- Combat
- Loot
- Bosses
- Crafting
- Gambling

Players use the Marketplace to:

- Sell Valuable Finds
- Purchase Desired Items
- Convert Assets Into Gold
- Optimize Character Builds

The economy should be driven primarily by valuable items rather than direct gold transfers.

---

# Marketplace Structure

## Trading Method

All player-to-player trading occurs through the Marketplace.

There is no:

- Direct Trading
- Face-To-Face Trading
- Gold Trading

Every exchange passes through Marketplace systems.

---

# Economic Separation

## Realm Isolation

Separate economies exist for:

### Seasonal Realm

Seasonal characters trade only with:

- Seasonal Characters

---

### Non-Ladder Realm

Non-Ladder characters trade only with:

- Non-Ladder Characters

---

## Rules

Items may never move between economies through Marketplace activity.

Each economy remains isolated.

---

# Tradable Objects

## Equipment

Players may buy and sell:

- Weapons
- Helmets
- Armor
- Shields
- Legs
- Boots
- Rings
- Amulets

---

## Materials

Players may buy and sell:

- Crafting Materials
- Gathering Materials
- Boss Materials

---

## Consumables

Players may buy and sell:

- Potions
- Boosts
- Blessings
- Protection Stones

---

## Unique Items

Unique Items are fully tradable.

Players may:

- Buy
- Sell
- List

without restrictions.

---

## Set Items

Set Items are fully tradable.

Players may:

- Buy
- Sell
- List

without restrictions.

---

# Non-Tradable Objects

The following may never be traded:

- Gold
- Characters
- Spells
- Achievements
- Achievement Score
- Holy Grail Progress
- Character Progression

These systems remain permanently bound to their owners.

---

# Gold Transfer Rules

## Overview

Players cannot transfer gold directly.

---

## Gold Movement

Gold enters another player's possession only through:

Marketplace Transactions

---

## Purpose

This prevents:

- Gold Gifting
- Gold Laundering
- Easy Abuse
- Unregulated Transfers

All economic exchanges remain tied to item value.

---

# Listings

## Active Listing Limit

Each character may maintain:

- 10 Active Listings

Maximum.

---

## Listing Duration

Players may select:

- 6 Hours
- 12 Hours
- 24 Hours

Maximum Duration:

24 Hours

---

# Listing Fee

## Cost

Creating a listing costs:

5% Of Asking Price

---

## Minimum Cost

Minimum Fee:

100 Gold

---

## Payment Rules

The fee is paid immediately.

The fee is never refunded.

---

## Economic Role

Listing fees function as a permanent gold sink.

---

# Sale Tax

## Overview

There is no sale tax.

Only listing fees remove gold from the economy.

---

The Marketplace exists to create:

Find Valuable Loot

↓

List Item

↓

Player Purchase

↓

Gold Earned

↓

Invest In Character

The Marketplace should support:

- Player-Driven Value
- Economic Specialization
- Long-Term Item Hunting
- Build Optimization
- Meaningful Trade

The primary Marketplace product should always be:

Equipment

followed by:

- Materials
- Consumables
